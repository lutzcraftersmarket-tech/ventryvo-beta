VENTRYVO is an upcoming event-management platform being built for craft markets, vendor events, festivals, pop-ups, farmers markets, holiday events, and other vendor-based events.

I’m looking for event coordinators to beta test:

  • Vendor applications, approvals, declines, and wait lists
  • Vendor databases and complete vendor history
  • Booth inventory, category limits, and multi-event management
  • Invoices, payments, monthly billing, credits, and cancellations
  • Check-in, attendance, no-show tracking, reports, and floor plans
  • Vendor portals, staff permissions, communications, and more

Selected testers will receive early access and will be asked to use VENTRYVO in real-world situations, report bugs, suggest improvements, and tell me what works—and what doesn’t.
